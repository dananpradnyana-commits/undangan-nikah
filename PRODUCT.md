# Product Context (`PRODUCT.md`)

- **Product:** Pawiwahan Dewa Dhar & Gek Cantik — Digital Balinese Hindu Wedding Invitation
- **Surface Mode:** Experience / Persuade (Interactive ceremonial wedding invitation with dual-stage cover reveal and RSVP guestbook)
- **Audience:** Invited wedding guests receiving personalized WhatsApp/social links (`?to=Krisna`) on mobile and desktop devices.
- **Core Journey:**
  1. Guest opens personalized link (`/?to=Krisna`) and sees the Welcome Cover (`CoverModal`) addressed directly to them (`Krisna`).
  2. Guest taps **Buka Undangan**, smoothly sliding away the cover, unlocking scroll, and starting romantic acoustic background music (`Jason Mraz - Let's See What the Night Can Do`).
  3. Guest experiences the Hero Save-the-Date banner with Ken Burns motion & live countdown, Rg Veda X.85.42 sacred blessing, Groom & Bride profiles, Event time/location with Google Maps navigation, 4 curated Masonry photo galleries with full-screen Lightbox & 3 pre-wedding films, interactive Buku Tamu (wishes & RSVP attendance), Instagram/Facebook AR filters, and Matur Suksma closing.
