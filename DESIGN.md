# Design System (`DESIGN.md`)

## Visual World & Aesthetic Direction
Warm Balinese Gold & Cream Romantic Editorial (`awsbali.com/dewadhar_gekcantik` faithful 1:1 reproduction). Combines dark cinematic full-bleed photography sections (`#000000` with Ken Burns motion and dramatic gradient vignettes) with warm ivory-cream (`#FFF8ED`) and soft peach (`#F3CBA8`) ceremonial cards accented by antique gold (`#A59071`, `#A48C4D`, `#EFBC72`, `#7B6945`).

## Typography
- **Script Display (`Dynalight`, 400):** Couple names (`40px` hero / `45px` section titles desktop, `35px` mobile), section headings (`Acara Kami`, `Galeri Wedding`, `Buku Tamu`, `Share Our Moment`, `Matur Suksma`).
- **Editorial Serif (`Lora`, 400/500/italic):** `PAWIWAHAN` (`26px` desktop / `20px` mobile), `save the date` (`letter-spacing: 3px`), Countdown digits (`35px` desktop / `25px` mobile), Rg Veda Sanskrit sloka (`17px` italic).
- **Slab Reference (`Crete Round`, 400):** `(RG VEDA X.85.42)` (`20px` uppercase, `#817556`).
- **Geometric Display Sans (`Montserrat`, 700):** Full Balinese names (`18px` desktop / `16px` mobile), event date (`18px`), Banjar venue (`28px` desktop / `20px` mobile).
- **Humanist Body Sans (`Poppins`, 400/italic/600):** Indonesian sloka translation, parental lineage, event hours, descriptions (`13px–15px` italic).
- **UI Controls (`Roboto`, 500 & `Playfair Display`, 400):** Action buttons (`View Map`, `Instagram filter`, `Facebook filter`) and footer credits.

## Color Tokens
- Surfaces: `#FFFFFF` (page base, patterned on mobile via `bg-pattern.png`), `#FFF8ED` (couple & guestbook cream surface), `#F3CBA8` (sloka frame), `#F5F3EE` (golden guestbook container), `#000000` (hero & footer).
- Accents: `#A59071` (primary antique gold script), `#A48C4D` (cover CTA button), `#EFBC72` (luminous gold on dark event overlay), `#7B6945` (guestbook button & links), `#C18C14` (RSVP confirmation badge), `#987527` (floating audio control).
